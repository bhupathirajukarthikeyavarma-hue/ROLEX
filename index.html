A story through time

More than a measure of time.

Since its beginnings in 1905, Rolex has become associated with watchmaking milestones, exploration and enduring design.

1905
The beginning

Hans Wilsdorf founded the company in London, beginning a journey that would shape modern wristwatchmaking.

1926
Oyster case

The Oyster case introduced a pioneering approach to water-resistant wristwatch construction.

1931
Perpetual rotor

The Perpetual rotor self-winding system became a defining chapter in the brand's technical story.
